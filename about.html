Our Origin & Love for Pets

Healthy Pets. Happy Homes.

Pet Shop was founded with a single mission: to provide pets and their owners with premium-quality food, cozy accessories, and professional grooming services that promote long, healthy, and happy lives.

The Founders' Journey

Caring for Every Pet

Pet Shop was founded in 2022 by animal nutritionists and pet enthusiasts Evelyn and Clara. Frustrated by the lack of premium, wholesome, and preservative-free options for their own pets, they set out to establish a neighborhood haven for pet care.

They partnered with premium local suppliers and organic pet food producers to curate a selection of grain-free food, durable toys, and hypoallergenic accessories. They also built a professional grooming spa to pamper pets in a calm, stress-free environment.

Today, we carry over 100 trusted brands and serve thousands of happy pets and their owners in our community.

Core Values

Our Three Commitments

Premium Sourcing

We source only from organic farms and trusted brands that prioritize natural, nutritious, and wholesome ingredients for pet foods.

Pet Comfort First

We select durable materials for accessories, ensuring all beds, cages, and toys are thoroughly tested for safety and pet comfort.

Community Support

We actively support local animal shelters by hosting weekly adoption events and giving starter kits to newly adopted pets.

Quality Assurance

Approved by Veterinarians

We collaborate with local veterinarians to ensure that our selection of foods, supplements, and grooming products meets the highest health standards. Your pet's well-being is always our primary concern.

We guarantee that all our treats are free from artificial preservatives, chemicals, and fillers. Only pure, delicious, and nutritious care goes into every bag.
