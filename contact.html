Pet Store FAQ

Quick FAQs

Do you ship internationally? +

Because our pet food and accessories vary in weight, we currently only ship within the contiguous United States. We cannot ship internationally at this time due to customs regulations on food and supplements.

How do I book a grooming appointment? +

You can book a grooming appointment by calling us directly, emailing our grooming desk, or filling out our contact form with "Grooming Appointment Inquiries". We suggest booking at least a week in advance!

What is your return policy? +

We offer a 30-day return policy on all unused accessories, toys, and unopened food products. If your pet didn't like a specific food, bring it back and our staff will help you find a replacement product!
