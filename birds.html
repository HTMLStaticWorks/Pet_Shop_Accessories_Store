Feeding Recommendations

Species-Specific Feeding for Optimal Health

Not all birds eat the same. A parrot's nutritional needs are vastly different from a canary's. Feeding an incorrect diet is the leading cause of preventable disease in pet birds. Our species-specific food ranges remove the guesswork.

Our avian nutrition team has curated food formulas for parrots, cockatiels, budgies, lovebirds, canaries, and finches — each formulated to deliver the correct protein ratios, vitamins, and mineral balance for each species.

  • Parrots & Macaws: 70% pellets + fresh vegetables + limited fruit treats
  • Cockatiels & Budgies: Quality seed mix + fortified pellets + leafy greens
  • Canaries & Finches: Canary grass seed + egg food + cuttlefish bone for calcium
  • All Birds: Daily vitamin supplement in water + mineral grit for digestion
