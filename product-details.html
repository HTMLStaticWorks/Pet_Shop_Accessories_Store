Orthopedic Benefits

Materials: 100% Medical-grade high-density orthopedic memory foam base, ultra-soft sherpa comfort top fabric, durable water-resistant inner lining, and non-slip bottom grip studs.

*All materials are certified non-toxic and pet-safe.

Size Guide

Choose the perfect fit for your pet
Small Size (Up to 15 lbs) 20" x 15" x 3"
Great for Chihuahuas, Pomeranians, and Cats.
Medium Size (Up to 40 lbs) 30" x 20" x 4"

Large Size (Up to 90 lbs) 40" x 30" x 5"
Great for Labradors, Retrievers, and German Shepherds.

The outer plush zippered cover is fully removable and machine-washable. Wash on cold-water delicate cycle and tumble dry on low heat.

The inner waterproof memory foam liner can be wiped down with a damp cloth if needed. Do not place the memory foam in the washing machine or dryer.

  • Pressure Relief: Thick memory foam conforms to your pet's body, easing pressure on aching joints and hips.
  • Waterproof Liner: Protects the memory foam from accidents, spills, and moisture, extending the life of the bed.
  • Improved Sleep: Relieves muscle tension and joint stiffness, leading to deeper, more restorative sleep cycles.
